How this map is organised

This map is divided into 5 ocean regions. You move through them in this order:

  1. Pacific Ocean
  2. Atlantic Ocean
  3. Indian Ocean
  4. Southern Ocean
  5. Arctic Ocean

Left or Up arrow goes to the previous ocean; Right or Down arrow goes to the next. Space or Enter plays the selected ocean from left to right across the map. Home returns to the Pacific Ocean.
